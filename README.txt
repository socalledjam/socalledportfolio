HOW TO PUT THIS SITE ONLINE (free)

Keep index.html and the videos folder together. Do not rename them.

Netlify (easiest): go to app.netlify.com/drop, drag this whole folder onto the page, then rename the site to "socalledjam" in Site settings to get socalledjam.netlify.app.
Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages also work: upload the same two things (index.html and the videos folder).

To use your own domain (for example socalledjam.com), buy it from a registrar and connect it in your host's domain settings.
